Standard broker accounting from Alpaca's own records: cost basis is FIFO over every fill (Alpaca's method). Realized P&L comes only from shares actually sold or bought back; unrealized P&L is open shares × (mark − average cost), at Alpaca's marks from the same moment as the account value. Account P&L = realized + unrealized + dividends, fees & interest. Returns are price returns, signed for shorts. SPY rows are the market hedge and, from 16 Nov 2026, the S&P 500 core. Invested = |shares| × entry price; an order's amount = filled shares × fill price. The Excel ledger also keeps a week-by-week lot view, which is not realized P&L.
