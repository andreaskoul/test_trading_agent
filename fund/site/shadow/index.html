Shadow books

Books scored every week but never sent to Alpaca · notional $100,000 each · updated … · Trade monitor · Position ledger

How to read this page. Only the live fund trades the account; every other book is paper, scored the same way and never sent to Alpaca. Each section answers one question and compares a book only with what it is meant to beat, over the same dates. Live results are a few weeks old: no verdict before mid-April 2027 (about 6 months, at least 3 times the longest holding horizon), so until then differences are mostly noise. Backtests are labelled and never mixed with live weeks. Every book is named by what it does; the glossary at the bottom maps each name to its record and to the design documents.

Marked the way the scorer (score.py) scores each book: a week's weights enter at the execution day's official close and are held to the next execution day's close; costs are 5 bp per side on the change in weights; idle cash earns nothing here. The running week is marked at the latest close, or at Alpaca's latest trades during market hours. These are hypothetical books with no fills, so they differ from the account: in week 1 most of the real orders expired unfilled. SPY rows are each book's market hedge, except in the S&P 500 benchmark, which is simply 100% SPY held throughout (one 5 bp buy at the start). Return = signed price return (dividends included); amount = |weight| × the book's value at entry. The scorer's weekly results in fund-data performance/ stay the record.
