Operations · checking the runner and its jobs…

Six things are being tested. The live fund trades a paper account at Alpaca; everything else is paper only and never sent to the broker. Each test compares a model only with what it must beat, over the same dates, and says when and by what rule it will be decided. Live results are weeks old, so differences are still mostly noise: no verdict before mid-April 2027. Returns are cumulative over the dates shown; % per year appears from 26 weeks. Backtests are labelled and never mixed with live weeks. Models retired on 5 Nov 2026 are listed at the bottom.

How the lines are marked. The live fund and its control are the model's own value, marked at each close after 5 bp per side. The weekly news fund, the price-factor model and random picks are marked as the scorer scores them: a week's weights enter at the execution day's official close and are held to the next execution day's close, 5 bp per side on the change in weights, idle cash earning nothing; the running week is marked at the latest close, or at Alpaca's latest trades during market hours.
