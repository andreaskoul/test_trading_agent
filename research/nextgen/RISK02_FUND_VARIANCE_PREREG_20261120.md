## Fixed source and no-leakage contract

Source: `research/tree/L4_exploit/b0_audit_runner/weekly_b0_ledger.csv` on `research/tree-layer3`, blob `2313f28e85f973152094f423df16d5f492531b56`, 302 chronological weekly rows already reproduced against B0 exact. Use no downloaded data. For week t with simple fund NAV return r_t, duration d_t and pre-week T-bill rf_t, define cash-excess 7-day risk-normalized return x_t=(r_t - rf_t*d_t/360)*sqrt(7/d_t) and variance proxy v_t=x_t^2, floored to 1e-8 for log and QLIKE. This is a noisy **weekly squared-return proxy**, not intraday integrated RV and not a fund return generated by a new strategy. The decision at start of row t may use **only x_0...x_(t-1)**. Every forecast target is the arithmetic mean v_t..v_(t+3), with at least four future weeks. No future SPY regime, cash rate, or return enters a feature. Samples overlap for four weeks; use NW/HAC 8-lag or longer and report effective sample caveat.

**Phenomenon test before model choice:** from t>=52 to t<=298, compute V13_t=mean(v_(t-13):v_(t-1)). Form high/low state using only the median of V13 over the preceding 52 observations. Compare average next-4-week variance high versus low. Advance to richer regime modelling only if the high/low realised variance ratio >=1.4, both groups >=40 origins and HAC t>=2 for the difference, **or** a causal fixed EWMA forecast beats a causal trailing mean in QLIKE with HAC t>=2. This is descriptive reused history; no live trading consequence even on a pass. No tuning of the threshold to score.

**Simple forecast controls**, evaluated t>=52 and next-four-week RV target: trailing 52-week variance average and EWMA with λ=0.94 of squared weekly excess returns, computed only on weeks up to t−1. QLIKE loss L=V/F−log(V/F)−1; lower is better, with positive paired gain defined as benchmark QLIKE minus candidate QLIKE.

**Model escalation pre-fixed:** only if phenomenon evidence passes, evaluate one expanding, rolling-origin HAR log-variance forecast with 1-week, 4-week and 13-week lagged averages of v; target log(mean(v_t..v_(t+3))). Ridge penalty fixed at 20 on standardised regressors, intercept unpenalised, with target completed by decision t (origin u+3<t), at least 104 completed origins. Evaluation t>=130, same weeks/targets for HAR, EWMA and trailing-52 benchmark. No random train/test split, hyperparameter search, shuffled labels, regime fitting on test rows, or tuning on already-viewed return observations. Forecasts are clipped [1e-8,0.25] variance. Compare fixed HAR to EWMA and trailing-52 with paired QLIKE, squared-error risk and rolling calibration; HAC 8 lags and per-year attribution.

**Advancement gates:** (1) significant signal of variance persistence; (2) HAR reduces QLIKE vs strongest simple comparator by >=5% and paired HAC t>=2.0, without worsening tail-event forecast calibration; (3) actual strategy impact must be independently tested prospectively, with identical core+MASTER capital, financing, costs, and matched risks. Forecast improvement alone is not alpha, and a high volatility state is not a direction forecast. Any model development beyond this design uses DEVELOPMENT data ending <=2019 and nested chronological training, NOT fit/choice on 2020–25; log all models and parameter trials, minimum prior family count >=162 for any alpha promotion.

**Stop:** missing/invalid rows or source SHA mismatch -> stop. If no material conditional variance signal, do not run HAR or advance HMM/GBM/deep forecasting. If signal exists but HAR is not better, record the failure and defer model escalation; never change exposure. If a model improves risk QLIKE, keep as risk **shadow** only pending independently preregistered decision-value test.
