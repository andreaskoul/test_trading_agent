# JOINT-01 research plan and predeclared comparison boundaries

- Branch based on `research/tree-layer3`, separate from consolidated PRs #184–186 and the live fund; do not merge into main without owner approval.
- Risk training factor basis: 2016–2017; development score: 2018–2019. Fixed origin stride 21 sessions (monthly), 5-session realised-covariance target. Compare EWMA, high-dimensional POET, matrix HEAVY, realised-GARCH, particle Bayesian factor SV; option to run PyMC NUTS independently. Fixed K=4, 120 most complete stocks by *early-development* intraday availability. Track missing coverage and possible survivorship bias.

- Alpha: original `dev.npz` only, 2013–2019; annual expanding training, 2017–2019 validation, five-session outcomes matured six sessions before any model update. Compare constant conditional slope, Bayesian TVP (Kalman) with observation noise, softly-gated posterior ridge using fitted causal HMM probabilities, nonlinear PyTorch MoE with grouped-by-week validation/early stopping. Do not train original MASTER weights; never replace its signal with an unregistered alpha.
- Economic decision layer: Student-t posterior predictive scenarios, parameter uncertainty, conservative funding, expected costs/impact, hard matched-gross constraint, beta/dollar-neutral book, CVaR and a Wasserstein expected-return penalty; first step of receding-horizon policy only. Until original approved P9 policy and *causal return-unit* forecasts are confirmed, results are hypothetical decisions, **not** backtest performance.
- All parameters and families constitute research trials; log changes and later compare on development only. No research claim of OOS improvement from 2020–2026 reuse. Entire H9 experiment remains untouched; no forward record writes. No CIK join from outdated current-issuer mapping.
- Engineering tests use synthetic fixtures solely to validate mathematical/causal contracts, **not** to claim strategy efficacy. Results from real caches are to be reviewed before any changes in experiments or allocation rules.
