## 5. Environment and gotchas
* **Venv:** `~/.cache/quant-tf-venv` (arm64 Python 3.11), with torch 2.14.1 on **MPS**. The Apple Neural Engine
  is not usable from PyTorch. Pins are in `requirements-quant.txt`.
* **Chronos-2:**
  * call `.eval()` after fine-tuning, because MPS SDPA does not support dropout;
  * use `cross_learning` with batch 600.
* **MASTER checkpoints:** load with `torch.load(..., weights_only=False)`.
* **Look-ahead:** MASTER market features are scaled with training-row mean and sd only (an earlier version
  leaked).
* **`score`** accepts parts whose commit is an ancestor of the signed one (`git merge-base --is-ancestor`).
* **zsh:** quote git refs like `"$B:r"`, or zsh's modifiers eat them.
* **Secrets:** never open the runner key file. Key-needing commands go through
  `python3 ~/.claude/hooks/with_secrets.py '<cmd>'`.
* **Merges:** no merges to `main` Wed 21:00 → Thu 17:30 UTC, 15:30–17:30 UTC on Mon, Tue, Wed and Fri, or
  20:30–02:00 UTC daily. The owner merges.
* **Earlier artefacts:**
